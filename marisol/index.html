The Kitchen

Four blocks from the boats.

The Newport dory fleet has pulled boats onto the beach at the foot of the pier since 1891 — the last of its kind on the West Coast. Our chef walks down at dawn, buys what came up, and drives it four blocks to the kitchen.

That's the whole sourcing strategy.

Open kitchen · Red oak fire
