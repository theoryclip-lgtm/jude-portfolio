Not a chain. Not a trend factory.

Crew has been cutting hair for the same Costa Mesa neighborhood since 1994: kids' first haircuts, wedding-day updos, the standing six-week appointment you don't think twice about.

Our stylists handle everything from a precision dry cut to full balayage and color correction. Tell us what you actually want, and we'll tell you honestly what will work with your hair.

We've moved, across the street, literally. Find us in Lab Holdings' newest project, CampOut, just behind The Camp at 2944 Randolph Ave.
