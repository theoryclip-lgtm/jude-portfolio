07Frequently asked questions

Instagram shows personality and updates, but a website gives customers one clear place to see your services, check your hours, find your location, and book. It makes the business look established — and unlike a feed, it works for you while you sleep.

You see a redesigned homepage before you pay anything. If you want it live, it's a flat monthly rate that covers the build, hosting, and every edit after. No setup fee, no surprise invoices, cancel whenever.

The free mockup lands in a few days. Once you approve it, a full site is usually live within a week — because it's just me, there's no queue and no handoffs.

Even better — I'll rebuild it. Most local sites are slow, hard to book on, or invisible on a phone. I'll show you the new version first, side by side, and you decide.

That's home base, but the whole thing runs online — design, edits, updates, all remote. If you run a local business anywhere, I can build for you.
